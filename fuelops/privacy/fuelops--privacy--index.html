FuelOps collects no personal data. It has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party tracking. Nothing you enter leaves your device.

Data the app collects

None. FuelOps does not ask for a name, an email address, a phone number, or any other identifier. It does not request location, contacts, camera, microphone, or storage permissions beyond what the operating system requires to run the application itself.

Data the app stores

Calculations you choose to save, along with any tank or cart tag you attach to them, are written to the device's own application storage. This data stays on the device. It is not uploaded, backed up to a server operated by the developer, or shared with anyone.

Removing this data is done by deleting individual saved records inside the app, by clearing the application's storage in Android settings, or by uninstalling the app. Uninstalling removes all of it.

Network use

FuelOps performs all calculations on the device. It does not send calculation inputs or results anywhere, and it does not require a network connection to work.

Third parties

The app contains no advertising software development kits, no analytics software development kits, and no crash reporting service that transmits data to the developer. No data is sold or shared, because none is collected.

Distribution through Google Play means Google collects its own installation and device information under its own terms. That collection is Google's, not the developer's, and is described in the Google Play privacy documentation.

Children

FuelOps is a tool for fuel operations work and is not directed at children. As it collects no data, it collects no data from children.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of the app changes what it collects or stores, this page will be updated before that version is published, and the effective date above will change with it.
